You said you would rather do everything in one trip, and your fees make that obviously right: the

the same depot.** They are a different branch of the same research — Transvaal court files and
estates from the 1940s to the 1970s, perhaps eight to fifteen of them, all TAB, mostly WLD and TPD

**I do not have that list finalised today, and I do not want to hold you up.** So:

**By when would you need the additional references in order to include them on the 22nd?** If the

Two small things about those extra files, so you can judge:

- For the **court files** I want the summons or declaration setting out the grounds, and any order

Lastly, you mentioned you could also help with the CJC war-compensation files for Marico, the OD

## THINGS TO TELL HER NOT TO HUNT

This matters more than the additions. **Three references are doubtful and an hour spent on any of
them is an hour lost from the list above:**

- **TAB MHG O/2899** — carried for days as *Jan Gerthardus Lodewikus Booijsen, d. 25 May 1885,
  Vaalbank*. **It would not reproduce on NAAIRS on 16 September** under Booijsen, Booysen or Booijzen
  paired with Gerhardus, Lodewicus or Lodewikus. It may exist under an untried spelling, or the
  reference may be a rendering rather than a call number. **Not worth looking for.**
- **TAB MHG 52653** — already on her list and already flagged. Never found on microfilm, and carries
  no year suffix unlike the other five. **If it is not where it should be, leaving it is the right
  answer.**
- **The OD series generally** — OR numbers are correspondence-register numbers. The documents almost
  certainly exist; whether each pulls as a discrete item or sits inside a bound volume is unknown.
  **Her judgement on the day beats anyone's from here.**
