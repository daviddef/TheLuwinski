WHAT YOUR LAST ANSWER PRODUCED

First, you sent me the death index, ref. 246, for 1894-1908, which I had not asked for. My notes
recorded that no such index existed. THAT WAS WRONG AND HAS BEEN CORRECTED HERE.

Second, one of your staff read the "Tochter der…" line in marriage record no. 9/1874 that I had

Third, you wrote that the REGISTERS OF THE JEWISH RELIGIOUS COMMUNITY OF SZUBIN SURVIVE FOR THE
YEARS 1820-1847. That is the reason for this letter.

ONE QUESTION

I have found an index entry: ABRAHAM SCHMUL, born at Szubin in 1846, died at Koronowo in 1904,
father JULIUSZ, mother JETTE née MUSCHLL. The year 1846 falls inside your span of 1820-1847 — only
just.

    Would it be possible to obtain a scan or copy of the BIRTH RECORD OF ABRAHAM SCHMUL of
    1846 from the Szubin Jewish community registers?

    And if those registers are not digitised — what is the procedure, and what does a query
    covering a single year cost?

WHY THAT ONE RECORD

Because it settles two things at once.

I am looking for the parents of FRIEDERICA SCHMUL, born at Szubin about 1852 — outside your span.
Her birth record exists neither in the civil registers nor in the surviving community registers.
BUT IF ABRAHAM IS HER BROTHER, his birth record names the same parents.

And I currently hold the mother's name in THREE VERSIONS, not one of them from a register: the
index gives JETTE MACHOT, your archivist read JUTTA NÉE MACHOL, and the index to Abraham's death
gives JETTE MUSCHLL. The father's forename differs too: JUDA on the marriage record, JULIUSZ on the
death index. A BIRTH RECORD OF 1846 WOULD GIVE BOTH IN A REGISTRAR'S HAND rather than an indexer's.

If a MARRIAGE RECORD OF JUDA AND JUTTA survives in the same 1820-1847 span it would be better
still — but I am not asking for it, because I do not know the year and that would be a search
rather than a single record.
