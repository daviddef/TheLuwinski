Subject: Thank you — Wear / Robertson, 7 September 1940

Dear Arlene,

Thank you. You answered every one of my five questions, including the one I most wanted a different
answer to, and you did it in a week with the page itself attached. That is more than I expected and
more than I had any right to.

Three things you have settled for me, and I want to say so plainly so you know the trouble was not
wasted:

    THE PARISH IS THE RIGHT ONE. You confirm Fr W. P. Whelan was parish priest at Rosebank from
    1935, so the single word "Rosebank" on a government form is no longer a guess on my part.

    THE REGISTER SURVIVES AND THE ENTRY IS IN IT. I had no way of knowing that from here.

    AND THE PARENTS ARE NOT NAMED. That is the answer I was hoping against, but a clear No from
    someone who has looked is worth a great deal more to me than an open question. It means I can
    stop looking down this road and put my effort somewhere else, which is its own kind of help.

I am grateful too for the explanation that the Church keeps a single marriage register and that the
banns are recorded within it. I had assumed there would be a separate book and would have gone on
asking you for one.

I have nothing further to ask. If anything about this couple should ever surface in the parish's
papers I would of course be glad to hear of it, but please do not go looking on my account.

Thank you again for your kindness, and please pass my thanks to whoever went to the register.

With best wishes to you and the parish,
