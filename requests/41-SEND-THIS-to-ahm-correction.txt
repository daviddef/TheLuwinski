It is therefore not the original entry.
  - It names her parents as JOAQUIM JÚLIO DE FIGUEIREDO, native of the parish of Vimieiro, and LEONIA DOS SANTOS MATA, native of the parish of Negozela; her paternal grandparents as ANTÓNIO JÚLIO DE FIGUEIREDO and ANA PEREIRA DE FIGUEIREDO; and her maternal grandparents as AUGUSTO MATA and JÚLIA DURÃES MATA.
  - It carries three marginal notes: her marriage to Frederico Pinto da Rocha on 18 November 1939 at the 1st Civil Registry Office of Lourenço Marques; the divorce, by sentence of 6 December 1945; and the rectification of her name to "Ivone Mata de Figueiredo".
