NOTE TO DAVID BEFORE SENDING — this one is new ground and it is cheap.

The BUNDESZENTRALKARTEI (BZK) is a NATIONAL card index of compensation claims under the
Bundesentschädigungsgesetz, held by the Bezirksregierung Düsseldorf. Every western Bundesland ran
its own compensation authority and § 185 BEG decided which one was competent in a given case —
so for a man who left Berlin in 1933 and lived thirty-six years in Johannesburg, WHICH AUTHORITY
HE FILED WITH IS NOT OBVIOUS, and Berlin may not be the answer.

The BZK answers the question that comes BEFORE Berlin's: did this person ever file at all, and
if so, where. It covers all four people in one enquiry.

It is a new thread to a body this archive has never written to — send it on its own, not as a
reply to anything. There is a Merkblatt at
brd.nrw.de/document/20230201_1_15_BZK_Merkblatt_deutsch.pdf if they ask for a particular form.
