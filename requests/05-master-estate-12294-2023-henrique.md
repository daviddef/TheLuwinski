# Request 5 — The estate file of Henrique da Rocha, and the document that names his father

**This is the most likely single answer to open question 8**, and it is in South Africa rather than
Portugal or Mozambique.

## What has been found

Searching **Gazettes.Africa** (free, full-text) for *Da Rocha Henrique* returns the estate notice,
gazetted three times as the law requires:

| Gazette | Date | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Government Gazette, Legal Notices A, no. **48890** part 1 | 7 July 2023 | 109 |
| Government Gazette, Legal Notices A, no. **49326** part 1 | 22 September 2023 | 166 |
| Government Gazette, Legal Notices A, no. **49462** part 1 | 13 October 2023 | 153 |

> **Estate 12294/2023 — (2) DA ROCHA, HENRIQUE FIGUEIREDO PINTO**

The name is exact — *Henrique Figueiredo Pinto da Rocha* — and the estate was opened in 2023 on a
death of **27 October 2022**, which is ordinary timing. **This is him.**

## Why it answers the question

A South African deceased estate file contains the **Death Notice, form J294**, and that form asks for:

- full names and surname of the deceased
- date and place of birth, identity number
- **the full names of the deceased's FATHER**
- **the full names and maiden name of the deceased's MOTHER**
- marital status, surviving spouse, children
- date, place and cause of death

The family tree can only call Henrique's father **“Unknown Da Rocha.”** This form names him. It will
also independently give **Ivone**'s name as his mother — from a document created in 2022–23 by people
who knew the family, eighty-one years after his birth.

## What to ask for

> **Master of the High Court — Deceased Estates**
>
> Estate late **DA ROCHA, HENRIQUE FIGUEIREDO PINTO**, estate number **12294/2023**, died
> **27 October 2022**.
>
> I request a copy of the **Death Notice (J294)** and the **Inventory** from this estate file.
> I am a family researcher; the deceased was the half-brother of my [relative]. I attach proof of
> identity and am willing to pay the prescribed copying fee.

**Which Master's office:** the estate number carries no office prefix in the gazette entry, so
establish it first. Estates of Johannesburg residents go to the **Master of the High Court,
Johannesburg**; Pretoria and Bloemfontein both appear on the same gazette pages for neighbouring
estates, so do not assume. The Master's offices can be asked by email, and **quoting 12294/2023 is
enough for them to locate it**.

## Two things worth doing at the same time

1. **Ask for the executor's details.** The gazette line names the executor and address after the
   estate number; the snippet index truncates before it, so the executor is not yet known. The
   executor — or the attorney — will hold the family's own account of who Henrique's parents were.
2. **His marriage record, 18 May 1968**, to Dawn Elizabeth Hendey. South African marriage registers of
   1968 — unlike the 1948 form, which has no parents column at all — do carry parents' particulars.
   That is a second, independent route to the same name.
