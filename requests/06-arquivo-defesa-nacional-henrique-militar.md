# Request 6 — Henrique's Portuguese military record

**The family's recollection is that Henrique served in the Portuguese army.** The dates make it very
plausible, and a Portuguese military registration **names both parents** — so if the record exists it
answers open question 8 from the Portuguese side.

| | |
|---|---|
| Born | **11 March 1941**, Lourenço Marques, Moçambique |
| Turned 20 — the conscription age | **11 March 1961** |
| The Colonial War began | **4 February 1961**, Angola — five weeks earlier |
| Left for Johannesburg | **November 1962**, aged 21 |
| Died | 27 October 2022, South Africa |

A *registo militar* / *caderneta militar* gives name, date and place of birth, **father's name**,
**mother's name**, parish, residence, occupation, physical description and unit.

## Where to send it, in order

| Repository | Address | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Arquivo da Defesa Nacional (ADN)**, Lisboa | `arquivo-adn.defesa.gov.pt` | Post-1911 military administration, including the Colonial War. **Most likely holder.** Send here first |
| **Arquivo Histórico Militar (AHM)**, Lisboa | **ahm@mail.exercito.pt** · `ahm-exercito.defesa.gov.pt` | Takes written requests quoting name and reference; holds *processos individuais*, some Mozambique-specific. Copies are charged for |
| **Arquivo Histórico de Moçambique**, Maputo | takes requests online | Inherited the colonial holdings in 1975. Where a territorially-raised conscript's file may well have stayed |

**Do not bother with Projecto GERMIL** (`ahmgermil-exercito.defesa.gov.pt`) — it is the *livros
mestres*, 1763–1908, and stops thirty years too early.

**And a negative already established:** Gazettes.Africa, which found the South African estate,
holds **no colonial Mozambican gazettes** — its Mozambique run begins in 1975. The *Boletim Oficial
de Moçambique*, which published call-up and posting lists, is not searchable there.

**The Maputo letter is not the same letter, deliberately.** This archive established in
`mozambique-sources.md` that the AHM's civil registers run **1865–1934**, and that Henrique's 1941
birth falls outside them. Writing to ask for a birth certificate would have wasted their time and
ours. That letter therefore opens by saying so, and then asks the different question: whether the
**distritos de recrutamento e mobilização** records of the 1950s and 60s are in their custody,
whether that documentation stayed in Mozambique after 1975, and what a search by name would cost.

The Lisbon letters differ from each other too — the AHM one says plainly that **GERMIL is known to
stop at 1908**, so they need not point there, and that the ADN has had the same request the same day.
Each archive is also told about the others, so that none of them does work another has already done.

All three state the reason outright rather than burying it: **o nome do pai**. The Portuguese
military register names the conscript's father and mother, and that is the only reason for writing.

## Honest expectation

There is **no online name index** for 1961 Mozambique conscripts, so this is a letter into an archive
rather than a search. It may take months and may come back empty.

**Do it in parallel with [Request 5](05-master-estate-12294-2023-henrique.md), not instead of it.**
