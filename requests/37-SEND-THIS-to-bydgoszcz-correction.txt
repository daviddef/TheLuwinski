WHY THIS LETTER EXISTS, AND IT IS NOT A THANK-YOU NOTE ALTHOUGH IT OPENS AS ONE:

  1. THE LETTER OF 22 SEPTEMBER TOLD THEM TWO THINGS THAT ARE WRONG. It gave the marriage as
     "24 listopada 1874" — the register says DECEMBER — and named the groom's mother as
     "WILHELMINY z domu POLLNOW", a reading this archive has since withdrawn entirely. If anyone
     at Bydgoszcz searched on either, they searched for something that does not exist. A state
     archive should not be left holding my errors in its case file.

  2. IT THANKS THEM FOR SOMETHING THEY WERE NOT ASKED FOR. They sent sygn. 246, the alphabetical
     index to the Szubin death register 1894-1908 — a volume this archive had published as
     non-existent on the strength of the portal's facets. Saying so plainly is both honest and
     the best way to make the next question worth their time.

  3. THE NEW QUESTION IS SMALL AND FOLLOWS FROM THEIR OWN GIFT: is there an equivalent index for
     1874-1893? That is the window Sally Leibholz is most likely to have died in, and sygn. 246
     does not cover it.
