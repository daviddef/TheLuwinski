## Why she is the route at all
Ms Xaba, National Archives Reading Room, 14 September:
> *"All the estate files you are requesting are **bound files**. Unfortunately, we are therefore
> unable to scan these files and provide complete digital copies by email."*

**MHG estate files cannot be got at from a distance.** They need a person in the building.
That is a constraint on this archive that no amount of searching gets around.

## What was added

**1. TAB MHG 10245/69 — WEAR, HENRY ROBERT.** Died Johannesburg 11 September 1969, aged 80,
buried West Park. **The death notice only**, and specifically its list of children — which is
the document that would test the whole Letchworth reconstruction against a South African
record. Flagged explicitly as *a different family from the other six*, same depot, same visit,
and as **the least important of the seven** — to be dropped rather than laboured over.

**2. The Deeds Registry — a question, not a commission.** Two ante-nuptial contracts,
both Johannesburg:
- one executed shortly before the marriage of **19 June 1948** (Kurt and Ivone)
- one executed shortly before the marriage of **February 1956** (Kurt and Pauline)

Both civil registers record the marriages as entered into **WITH** an antenuptial contract, so
the deeds exist. A deeds registry will not act on a letter or a telephone call, which is the
obstacle this archive has been stuck behind for weeks.

**It is a different institution from the archives** — a Deeds Registry, not the Reading Room —
so the question asked was whether she does that work *at all*, with an explicit offer to take
a name instead if it is outside her line.
