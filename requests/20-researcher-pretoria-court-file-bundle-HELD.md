## Why one bundle and not eight letters

Between 15 and 16 September this archive found **eight court and estate files in one depot** —
the Transvaal Archives Repository (TAB) in Pretoria. They come from five different lines of enquiry
and were opened as five separate work-list rows, which is how research actually happens and is a
terrible way to commission it. **One person, one visit, one list.**

Ms Xaba's constraint from 14 September governs all of it:

> *"All the estate files you are requesting are **bound files**. Unfortunately, we are therefore
> unable to scan these files and provide complete digital copies by email."*

## Two things the brief must say plainly

**1. These are two different families.** The Wear files and the Luwinski/Salkow files belong to one
research project but to two unrelated surnames, and the researcher should not waste time looking for
a connection between them. There is none, except the man this archive is trying to identify.

**2. Not every file needs copying in full.** For the estates, **the death notice is the document**;
for the divorces, **the summons**. A bound file photographed cover to cover costs more and says less.
If pages have to be chosen, choose those.
