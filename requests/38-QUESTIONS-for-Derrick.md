# Questions for Derrick

Every question here exists because a document could not answer it and he can. They are ordered so
the most important come first, and the ones he may not wish to answer are marked and put last.
Nothing here needs answering in order, or at all. A "don't know" is a real answer and will be
recorded as one — this archive would rather print *not established* than a guess.

---

## 1. Kurt — the gap the tree page names out loud

The record says Kurt Israel Luwinski married Ivone in June 1948 and filed for divorce the same
year, **within months of the wedding and before the child was born**. It also says he died in
**Johannesburg on 15 December 1968** — the same city, for twenty years. The archive therefore does
not claim he raised Derrick, and it does not claim he didn't. It says nothing, because nothing has
been read either way. **This is the single largest hole in the whole archive and one conversation
closes it.**

1. Did you know Kurt? Did you see him, and how often?
2. Did he support you or your mother — money, visits, letters, anything?
3. When did you last see him, and did you know he had died in 1968?
4. Who did you understand your father to be, as a child? Was it ever discussed at home?
5. Did your mother talk about him? In what terms?
6. Is there anything of his — a photograph, a letter, a document, an object?

## 2. The Berlin family, who were alive for most of your life

This is the part the archive did not expect. Kurt's family did not all die in the war.

- **Auguste "Lizzy" Luwinski**, his mother — your grandmother of record — survived and died at
  **Kraichtal, Germany, on 22 June 1975**.
- **Siegfried Leibholz**, his brother — your uncle of record — was hidden somewhere near Sommerfeld
  from January 1943 to April 1945, survived, and died on **1 February 2005**.
- **Pauline**, Kurt's last wife, died in **1985**.

7. Did you ever meet or hear from Auguste, your grandmother? Did she know about you?
8. Did you know Siegfried, or of him? Did anyone in the family ever speak about the boy who was
   hidden?
9. Was there contact between Johannesburg and Germany at all — letters, parcels, a visit either way?
10. Did anyone ever tell you what happened to Kurt's father, Jacob, or to his uncles?

## 3. What was known in the family, and when

The archive has reconstructed the deportations in detail. What it cannot reconstruct is what the
family itself knew, and what was said or not said.

11. Growing up, did you know the Luwinski name was half a Jewish family's? Was that spoken about?
12. Did you know your grandfather Jacob Leibholz was murdered at Auschwitz, and if so, when did you
    learn it, and from whom?
13. Was there any observance, any memorial, any date kept?
14. Did you know Kurt's middle name was **Israel**, and that it was forced on him by a German decree
    in 1938 rather than given to him? Was that ever explained?

## 4. Your mother, and Portugal

15. What did Ivone tell you about her own parents — Joaquim Júlio de Figueiredo and Leónia? Did you
    ever meet them or see a photograph?
16. Did she talk about Lourenço Marques? Did she ever go back?
17. Do you know why she went to Portugal at the end, and when?
18. Was the surname **Mota** or **Mata**? Her birth entry and her death certificate disagree, and
    the archive has flagged it rather than choosing. Did she ever write it herself?
19. Who was with her at Gulpilhares at the end, besides Jacinto?

## 5. Henrique, and the papers he passed you

Your half-brother **Henrique Figueiredo Pinto da Rocha** (born 11 March 1941, died 27 October 2022)
is the reason the Portuguese documents exist here at all.

20. How much contact was there between you and Henrique over the years?
21. What did he tell you about the family in Portugal, or about his own father, Frederico Pinto da
    Rocha?
22. Do you know how he came to have the papers — were they sent to him, or did he collect them?
23. Is there more of that bundle? The fax of 16 March 2010 clearly carried more sheets than the ones
    photographed, and one of them may be the **1939 marriage entry** this archive is still chasing.

## 6. Things rather than memories

24. Photographs — of Kurt, of Ivone, of anyone. Even unlabelled ones; this archive can often date a
    photograph from what is in it.
25. Any paper at all with a letterhead, an address, a reference number or a stamp.
26. Addresses you remember living at in Johannesburg, and roughly when.
27. Anything in German, Portuguese or Polish that nobody could read and so nobody threw away.

## 7. Harder ones — skip freely

These touch on things that may be private or painful. They are asked because the archive already
publishes the findings and would rather have his words than its own inferences. **No answer is
needed and none will be chased.**

28. When did you learn that Kurt was not your biological father, and how?
29. Is Leonard Wear a name you knew, or one this archive put in front of you?
30. Is there anything published on the site you would rather was not there? *The standing rule is
    that anything comes down on asking, without argument and without a reason being given — and that
    includes his own name.*
