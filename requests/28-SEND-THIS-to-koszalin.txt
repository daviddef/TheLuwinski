Dear Sir,

I confirm receipt of your letter of 16 September 2026, reference O-S.6342.406.2026. Thank you for a prompt and unambiguous answer.

A negative answer is as valuable to me as a positive one, for a particular reason. GEORG LEIBHOLZ was born at Czarne (Hammerstein) on 7 June 1884 — the Bundesarchiv Gedenkbuch says so, entry en1100801. The family record has him as the brother of JACOB LEIBHOLZ, born at Szubin (Schubin, Posen) on 8 August 1884. Two men cannot be brothers, born two months apart in two different provinces.

An 1884 birth entry from the Czarne registry office was the only document that could settle it, by naming Georg's parents. Your letter tells me that document is not available, so my conclusion rests on the Gedenkbuch. That is exactly what I needed to know: there is no point looking further down this road.

I am particularly grateful that you wrote plainly that you hold no information about the fate of these books or where they might now be kept. Many archives stop at "we do not have it". Your phrasing tells me a great deal more — that the question of where they are is open, and that I should not assume anybody in Poland knows the answer.

I am not asking for anything further. You will not need to spend any more time on me.
