## Why these four, and in this order
The Wear death notice is the document that would test — against a South African record —
a reconstruction built entirely from English birth, census and marriage indexes. If it names
his children, the Letchworth family is confirmed from the South African end.

The three court files are the only contemporaneous account of what happened inside the 1948
marriage. A child was born between the 1948 action and the 1951 one who is alive today, and the
DNA says Kurt was not his father. **The grounds pleaded in 1950/1948 are the nearest thing to
testimony this archive will ever have.**

## What was asked for, deliberately
- From the estate: **one page**, not the file. Whole-file requests are slower and likelier
  to be refused.
- From the court files: the **summons or declaration setting out the grounds**, and any order
  — with an explicit offer to accept a note of the grounds and outcome instead, if the files
  are bulky. Asking for less makes it likelier that something comes back.
