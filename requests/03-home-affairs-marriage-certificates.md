# Request 3 — The two Johannesburg marriage certificates

South African marriage certificates of this period name **the fathers of both parties**. Two are
wanted, and both are already located to the image.

| | |
|---|---|
| **19 June 1948**, Johannesburg | Kurt Luwinski & **Ivone de Figueiredo de Witt**. Certificate **858**. FamilySearch digital folder **008109682**, image **2045** |
| **13 February 1953**, Johannesburg | Morris Isaac Salkow & **Ivone de Figueredo de Witt Luwinski**. Certificate **146**. FamilySearch digital folder **008117293_003_M968-8S4**, image **397** |

**Two routes, and the first is free.**

1. **Look at the images.** They are already digitised. A free FamilySearch account, possibly viewed
   from an affiliate library, may be enough to open them. This costs nothing and should be tried
   before anything is ordered. *Try this first.*
2. **Order unabridged certificates** from the South African Department of Home Affairs
   (form BI-130, *Application for Marriage Certificate*), quoting the certificate numbers and date.
   The **unabridged** form is the one that carries parents' details — the abridged one does not, so
   the form must say so explicitly.

Also outstanding at the national archives, and free to search: **43 documents indexed under
FIGUEIREDO** in NAAIRS, none yet read. The interface serves one document per request and began
timing out; it wants a patient half-hour rather than a clever script.
