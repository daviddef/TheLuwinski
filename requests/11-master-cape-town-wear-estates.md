**Why these two.** The DNA places Derrick's biological father among the sons of Henry Robert
Wear and Martha Maud Castle, and the only son of the right age is **Leonard Aubrey Robert
Wear** (b. Hitchin 1917, in Johannesburg from 1940). A seven-person MyHeritage tree kept by
his own daughter gives his mother as *"Muad Wear, born Castle"* — the first independent
confirmation of the Castle link, and the only source anywhere that gives Leonard parents.

But a user-submitted tree is not a document. **A death notice is.** Every South African
deceased-estate file contains **Form J294**, which has fields for the deceased's
**father's full names** and **mother's full names and maiden name**.

Two of Leonard's presumed daughters died in the Cape and their estates are both under the
Master at Cape Town. **If either J294 names LEONARD AUBREY ROBERT WEAR as father, the whole
reconstruction is confirmed from a government record** — and the sisterhood of these women is
established at the same time.
