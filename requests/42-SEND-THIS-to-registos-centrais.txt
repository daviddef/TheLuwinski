Subject: Request for certificates — the marriage of 18 November 1939 (Lourenço Marques) and the birth of Henrique Figueiredo Pinto da Rocha (1941)

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing from Australia, as part of family genealogical research that is not commercial. The request has two parts; if either is outside your competence, I should be grateful to be told which body to approach.

FIRST — THE MARRIAGE

FREDERICO PINTO DA ROCHA and IVONE MATA DE FIGUEIREDO were married in a civil ceremony on 18 November 1939 at the 1st Civil Registry Office of Lourenço Marques, Mozambique. The marriage was dissolved by divorce, by sentence of 6 December 1945.

I know this from two marginal notes to the bride's birth entry, no. 447-D, folio 224, of your Conservatória dos Registos Centrais, made on 7 May 1987 (the notes were entered on 7 May and 30 September 1987; her name was rectified on 9 October 1987, file 10.277/87). She was born at Lourenço Marques on 4 March 1922 and was also recorded as Ivone Mota de Figueiredo, Ivone de Figueiredo and Yvonne de Figueiredo.

I request a full-text certificate of the marriage entry, with every marginal note, the names of the parents of both parties and those of the witnesses. If the entry was not transcribed into your office, I should be grateful to be told where the original is.

SECOND — THE SON'S BIRTH

HENRIQUE FIGUEIREDO PINTO DA ROCHA, born on 11 March 1941, presumably at Lourenço Marques, a son of Ivone, born therefore sixteen months after the marriage. He died at Johannesburg, South Africa, on 27 October 2022.

I request a full-text certificate of the birth entry, which should identify the father and the grandparents.

I understand that a record less than a hundred years old may have restricted access. The person registered was a son of Ivone, and my father-in-law is also her son, that is, the half-brother of the person registered; I am married to a daughter of his. I should be grateful to know what proof of kinship or legitimate interest is needed. My father-in-law can sign the request or an authority, if that is preferable.

TO HELP THE SEARCH

The surname of Ivone's mother reads MATA in the 1987 entry and MOTA in other documents, and Ivone's own name varies (Figueiredo, de Figueiredo, de Figueredo). If a search under one fails, please try the others.

I should also be grateful to know the cost of each certificate, how payment can be made from abroad, and whether the request should be made electronically rather than by email.

With best wishes and with my thanks,

David Defranceski
Brisbane, Australia
david.defranceski@gmail.com
