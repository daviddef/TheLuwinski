Dear ——,

I wrote a few days ago about the DNA match between you and Derrick Luwinski, and I
said then that I would send you anything I found rather than only ask.

I have found something, and it is about your Castle line.

Your tree has **George William Castle and Caroline** with five children — George,
Ada, Frederick, Agnes Kate and Martha. I went looking for them in the censuses and
they are all in one house: **Norton End, Norton, Baldock, Hertfordshire** in 1901,
and **the Church Yard, St Mary's Cottages, Baldock** in 1911. George was a farm
labourer and later worked in the brewery; they married in 1883.

Two things in the 1911 return are not in your tree, and you may want them:

- **two more children — Hilda, born 1902, and Ernest, born 1904**
- **Caroline's father is living with them: Joseph Walls, 84, a widower, born at
  Weston.** So Caroline was born a **Walls**, not a Wallis.

And **Martha** is no longer untraced. She married **Henry Robert Wear** at Hitchin in
the last quarter of **1914**; by 1921 they were at 72 Pix Road, Letchworth, and he
was a machine minder at J. M. Dent's printing works. Their youngest daughter,
**Cynthia Yvonne Elizabeth Wear, born 1931**, went to South Africa and married in
Johannesburg in 1952 — and *her* granddaughter is Derrick's closest DNA match of all,
at 297.5 cM. So you and she are both descended from George and Caroline, through two
different daughters.

That is why I am writing. Derrick shares 292.7 cM with you and 297.5 cM with her —
almost exactly the same, down two separate branches of the same family. That pattern
does not happen unless he comes from inside that household rather than from beside it,
and the arithmetic puts him one generation higher than I first thought: his father was
most likely **a son of George and Caroline**, not a grandson.

So the one question, and it is a narrow one:

> **What do you have on the Castle *sons* — George W. born 1885, Frederick born 1890,
> and Ernest born 1904? Who did they marry, and is there any hint that any of them, or
> any of their children, went out to South Africa?**

In June 1948 one of that family was in Johannesburg. I would like to know which.

Anything at all — a marriage, a death, a note in the margin of the tree, a name you
were told as a child — would help. And if you would rather I sent you the census
images and the index entries so you can judge them yourself, say the word and they
are yours.
